# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Additional Project Roles

These roles make ownership clear across planning, delivery, measurement, and release. They describe accountabilities, not required headcount; teams may combine roles based on project size and needs.

### Engineering / Technical Leads

#### Role Summary
Engineering or Technical Leads guide technical decisions and help the delivery team build a feasible, maintainable solution.

#### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Coordinate technical estimates, dependencies, and engineering risks
- Support consistent engineering practices and implementation quality

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Project Manager on sequencing, estimates, dependencies, and technical risks
- Work with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and explain technical trade-offs
- Guide Developers through design and implementation, and collaborate with QA/Testing on testability and technical quality
- Explain significant technical decisions and impacts to Stakeholders as needed

### UX / Product Designers

#### Role Summary
UX or Product Designers shape usable, accessible experiences based on customer needs and product goals.

#### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and map workflows
- Develop and validate interaction designs and prototypes
- Identify usability and accessibility requirements

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with the Product Manager to connect user needs to outcomes, scope, and acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers to assess design feasibility and with QA/Testing to make experience requirements testable
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on design activities and dependencies, and use Stakeholder input to validate needs

### Data / Analytics Leads

#### Role Summary
Data or Analytics Leads ensure project outcomes can be measured with appropriate, reliable data.

#### Responsibilities
- Define measurement plans, metrics, and reporting needs
- Identify instrumentation, data-quality, and analytics dependencies
- Interpret results and communicate limitations in the data

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align success metrics and interpretation with the Product Manager
- Coordinate instrumentation and data quality with Developers, and validation with QA/Testing
- Work with the Project Manager to plan measurement dependencies and report progress; share outcome insights with Stakeholders

### Security / Privacy Leads

#### Role Summary
Security or Privacy Leads advise the team on protecting systems and data and meeting applicable security and privacy requirements.

#### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements, risks, and necessary assessments
- Recommend controls and mitigation plans
- Review evidence that required controls and assessments are complete

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advise Developers on implementing controls and QA/Testing on validating them
- Work with the Project Manager to track risks, mitigation owners, and escalations
- Partner with the Product Manager and relevant Stakeholders on requirements and risk trade-offs

### Release / Operations Leads

#### Role Summary
Release or Operations Leads coordinate production readiness and the operational handoff for a change.

#### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment readiness, monitoring, and rollback planning
- Confirm operational documentation and support handoffs are prepared
- Organize post-deployment verification and communicate operational issues

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Developers and QA/Testing on deployment plans, smoke tests, and verification
- Partner with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, and communications
- Align release scope and readiness expectations with the Product Manager, and share status with Stakeholders and support teams

### Customer Support / Success Representatives

#### Role Summary
Customer Support or Success Representatives bring customer-facing insight into delivery and prepare teams to support released changes.

#### Responsibilities
- Share customer needs, feedback, and recurring issues
- Identify support, training, and customer communication needs for upcoming changes
- Collect post-release feedback and route issues to the appropriate owners

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to inform priorities and assess customer impact
- Coordinate support readiness and stakeholder communications with the Project Manager
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify customer-facing acceptance needs and report defects or feedback

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
